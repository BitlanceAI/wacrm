First Contact
Getting
Started
A few steps connect your WhatsApp Business account and bring the CRM to life. Work them in order.
ROUTE /settings?tab=whatsapp  ·  SOURCE Meta  ·  TOKEN encrypted
  1. 01
    Sign in
    Log in with your email and password. First time here? Create your account on the sign-up screen.
  2. 02
    Connect WhatsApp
    Settings → WhatsApp: enter your Phone Number ID, WABA ID, Permanent Access Token, and a Verify Token from Meta. Save — your token is stored securely and reused on later edits.
  3. 03
    Set the webhook
    Copy the callback URL shown in Settings into Meta, match the verify token, and subscribe to messages. This is what delivers replies and read receipts back to you.
  4. 04
    Sync templates
    Your Meta-approved templates pull in automatically on connect, ready to use in the Inbox and Broadcasts.
  5. 05
    You're live
    Reply from the Inbox, add contacts, run a broadcast, and build your pipeline.

02
The Thread
Inbox
Every inbound and outbound message, gathered into a single live conversation per contact.
ROUTE /inbox  ·  REALTIME yes  ·  MEDIA proxied
  1. 01
    Send & receive
    Text, image, video, document, audio, and location — inbound and outbound — all render in the thread as they arrive.
  2. 02
    Templates in chat
    Open the template picker to send an approved template mid-conversation. It carries the correct language automatically.
  3. 03
    Reactions & replies
    React with an emoji, or swipe-reply to quote an earlier message. Reactions are stored apart from the message log.
  4. 04
    Signals
    Unread badges track the conversation list; delivery ticks advance sent → delivered → read as the webhook reports.
A conversation opens automatically on first inbound message.

The Register
Contacts
The people the system knows — each a record you can tag, extend, and trace across every other instrument.
ROUTE /contacts  ·  PHONE E.164  ·  IMPORT CSV
  1. 01
    Create & edit
    Add a contact with name, phone, email, and company. Store phone in full E.164 — for India, 91 + ten digits. A malformed number fails to reach WhatsApp.
  2. 02
    Import in bulk
    Upload a CSV and map its columns to fields. Rows arrive as contacts ready for tagging and broadcast.
  3. 03
    Tags
    Attach tags to segment audiences. Broadcasts and automations can target a tag directly.
  4. 04
    Custom fields
    Define your own fields for data the standard record omits. These become mappable variables in broadcast personalization.
  5. 05
    Detail view
    Open a contact to see notes, linked deals, and full history in one place.

The Board
Pipelines
Deals as cards, dragged by hand from stage to stage — with the value of the whole board read back to you in rupees.
ROUTE /pipelines  ·  CURRENCY INR  ·  VIEW Kanban
  1. 01
    Move deals
    Drag a card between columns to advance it. Stages are yours to name and order.
  2. 02
    The deal form
    Record title, value, contact, stage, and owner. Value is entered in INR; the whole app settles in rupees.
  3. 03
    Analytics
    Pipeline value, average deal size, and a weighted expected value — each open deal's worth times its stage probability. Lost deals are excluded.
  4. 04
    Win & loss
    Mark deals won or lost; the board re-derives its totals and the monthly won / lost counts on its own.

Bitlance · WhatsApp CRMField Manual§05 · Broadcasts
05
The Fan-out
Broadcasts
One approved template, personalized per recipient and sent at scale — then tracked as each message is delivered and read.
ROUTE /broadcasts/new  ·  STEPS template → personalize → audience → send
  1. 01
    Choose a template
    Pick an approved template. The list auto-syncs from Meta on connect and refreshes when stale — no manual sync for the common case.
  2. 02
    Personalize
    Drag a contact field, custom field, or static value onto each {{n}} slot. Every placeholder must be mapped before you continue.
  3. 03
    Audience & schedule
    Select recipients by tag or list; send now or schedule. Messages fan out in batches with per-recipient results.
  4. 04
    Track delivery
    The detail page shows sent / delivered / read / replied per recipient — populated by the webhook.
⚠ Field caution — error #132012

The number of values you send must match the template's {{n}} variables at Meta exactly. If the stored copy has drifted, sync it (Settings → Templates → Sync) before sending, or Meta will reject the parameter format.

06
The Rule
Automations
Trigger-driven sequences that act on your behalf — sending, tagging, waiting, and branching without a hand on the wheel.
ROUTE /automations  ·  ENGINE steps  ·  CRON waits
  1. 01
    Set a trigger
    An event starts the rule — an inbound message, a new contact, a tag applied. One trigger opens each automation.
  2. 02
    Chain steps
    Add steps in sequence: send a message, apply a tag, wait a duration, or branch on a condition.
  3. 03
    Activate safely
    Activation is validated — an incomplete configuration cannot go live, so a half-built rule never runs.
  4. 04
    Wait steps & cron
    Delays are drained by a scheduled pinger. Set the cron secret so paused executions resume on time.
  5. 05
    Logs & reuse
    Every run is logged per automation. Duplicate an existing rule to reuse its shape.

The Self-Running Conversation · Beta
Flows
A conversation drawn as a graph of nodes — menus, buttons, and questions that walk each contact through on their own.
ROUTE /flows  ·  NODES menu · buttons · input · condition · handoff
  1. 01
    Build on the canvas
    Lay out nodes and connect them. The engine auto-advances through send and condition nodes, and pauses at buttons, lists, and input.
  2. 02
    Start from a template
    Three prebuilt flows — welcome menu, FAQ bot, and lead capture — give you a working graph to adapt.
  3. 03
    One run per contact
    Each contact holds a single active run. Replies route by their button or list id; unmatched replies follow the fallback policy.
  4. 04
    Activate & observe
    Publish a flow, then watch executions in its runs history to see where contacts land.
Flows dispatch before automations on each inbound message.

The Instrument Panel
Settings &
Templates
Where the system is tuned — your identity, your WhatsApp connection, and the template catalog kept true to Meta.
ROUTE /settings  ·  TABS profile · whatsapp · templates · tags · appearance
  1. 01
    Profile & security
    Edit your name and avatar, change your password, and review active sessions.
  2. 02
    WhatsApp
    Enter and test Meta credentials. The access token is stored encrypted and reused — re-enter it only to rotate.
  3. 03
    Templates
    Meta is the source of truth. Sync pulls approved templates into the catalog; it runs on connect and when the copy is stale.
  4. 04
    Header type
    For templates you intend to broadcast from the app, keep Header = None — media headers are not yet sent.
  5. 05
    Tags & appearance
    Manage the tag vocabulary and switch the theme to taste.

The signals you may meet in the field, and the words behind them — read a symptom, apply the remedy.
SymptomMeaningWhat to do
No templates shownCatalog is empty.Add your WABA ID in Settings → WhatsApp, then press Sync. Only Meta-approved templates appear.
"Not Connected"WhatsApp not linked.Re-enter your credentials in Settings → WhatsApp and use Test Connection.
#132012Wrong variable count.The values you sent don't match the template's {{n}} at Meta. Sync the template, remap, and resend.
#132001Template not on Meta.The name or language isn't approved at Meta. Check both, or press Sync.
Can't save templateInvalid header type.Set Header to None, or a valid type (text · image · video · document).
Sent, never deliveredNumber or webhook.Confirm the contact's number is full E.164, and that the webhook is set up (§01).
WABA IDWhatsApp Business Account ID — required to sync templates. Distinct from the Phone Number ID.
PHONE NUMBER IDMeta's identifier for the sending number. Used on every send.
TEMPLATEA pre-approved message form. Created and approved at Meta; cached locally.
E.164Full international phone format — country code plus number, no symbols. India: 91 + ten digits.
